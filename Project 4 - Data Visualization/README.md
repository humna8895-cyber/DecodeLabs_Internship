# DecodeLabs Project 4 – Data Visualization

## Overview

This project focuses on transforming raw order data into clear and meaningful visualizations using Python.

The analysis was completed in **Google Colab** using **Pandas** and **Matplotlib**.

## Dataset

* **Records:** 1,200
* **Variables:** 14
* **Time period:** January 2023 – June 2025
* **Main analytical fields:** Date, Product, TotalPrice, OrderStatus

## Visualizations

### 1. Sales by Product

A horizontal bar chart comparing total sales across the seven products.

**Key insight:** Chair and Printer generate the highest total sales, while Phone has the lowest total sales.

### 2. Monthly Sales Trend

A line chart showing how total sales fluctuate month by month.

**Key insight:** Sales vary throughout the study period, with a peak month highlighted in the visualization.

### 3. Order Status Distribution

A horizontal bar chart comparing the number of orders across different order statuses.

**Key insight:** Cancelled and Returned orders represent a substantial share of the total orders.

### 4. Sales Contribution by Product

A horizontal bar chart showing each product's percentage contribution to total sales.

**Key insight:** The seven products contribute relatively similar shares of total sales.
